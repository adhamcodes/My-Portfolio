# Living Trace v2 direction

This temporary engineering note captures the Prototype 01 red-light revision target.

- Macro: one continuous identity spine with real dimensional mass.
- Meso: braided structural filaments and state-weighted branches.
- Micro: sparse moving signals and terminal landmarks only where state exists.
- Chapter context changes emphasis; it does not invent life-state.
- Dormant growth remains visibly unresolved rather than pretending to be progress.
- Pointer and scroll apply force to the field; they do not rotate a decorative object.
- No sphere, HUD, fake telemetry, generic network graph, or random nodes.
