# ADHAM — MASTER 2.0 V4 CONSTITUTION

**Status:** LOCKED FOR V4  
**Build branch:** `master-2-living-world-v4`  
**Production branch:** `main`  
**Promotion rule:** V4 may reach `main` only after Adham approves the complete owner-review build.

## 1. What this is

This is not a decorated résumé and not a website pretending to be a fictional operating system.

It is:

> **Adham Mahmood — an evolving digital representation of a person learning, building, shipping, and changing over time.**

The visitor should feel that they have entered a world being constructed in real time. The world must remain understandable as Adham's portfolio, but it should behave more like a place with memory, material, causality, and unfinished future structure than a sequence of web sections.

The permanent identity is not a visual asset. It is the relationship:

> **TRUTH / EVENTS → STATE → EXPERIENCE → WORLD**

Visual generations may be replaced. Verified memory survives.

## 2. The V4 synthesis

V4 combines two things that earlier generations separated:

- the prototype's immediate visual nerve and spatial confidence;
- V3's truthful architecture, restraint, accessibility, and long-lived state model.

The result must keep the old work's courage without restoring its sphere, HUD chrome, tiny telemetry, Deck, XRAY, decorative sound, or technology theatre.

The design thesis remains:

> **EDITORIAL MONUMENT × LIVING COMPUTATION × TIME**

The V4 world thesis is:

> **A LIVING MONUMENT UNDER CONSTRUCTION.**

It is monumental enough to feel authored, alive enough to react, and unfinished enough to change honestly with the person it represents.

## 3. The protagonist

Adham is the protagonist.

Not React. Not WebGL. Not AI. Not the renderer. Not GitHub activity. Not Foundry. The engineering should be experienced before it is explained.

The factual direction is:

> **SOFTWARE ENGINEERING FOUNDATION → AI / ML ENGINEERING DESTINATION**

AI automation is leverage and a supporting capability. Building projects across subjects is an ongoing hobby and passion. Foundry180 is the beginning of the formal learning path: its curriculum exists, while Adham's personal learner state remains **0 / 180** until verified learning events occur.

## 4. The world and its body

The old sphere and V3 ribbon are replaced by a world-scale identity system. It must have:

- **macro structure** — silhouette, architecture, void, terrain, impossible scale;
- **meso topology** — regions, branches, thresholds, project worlds, history layers;
- **micro matter** — signals, dust, filaments, stress, heat, residue, local computation.

The visual system is not an object placed next to the content. It is the environment's body. Removing it must materially destroy the experience.

Its state domains remain:

- **SELF** — current direction and focus;
- **WORK** — things that became real;
- **GROWTH** — verified learning and unresolved direction;
- **HISTORY** — meaningful residue that is never overwritten;
- **PRESENT** — the temporary equilibrium of all four.

## 5. Visitor law

The visitor is a temporary force, not an author of Adham's identity.

Pointer, touch, keyboard, and scroll may apply pressure, reveal depth, disturb matter, propagate through nearby structure, and settle. The response grammar is:

> **LOCAL FORCE → NEARBY PROPAGATION → SETTLING**

Visitor influence is ephemeral and session-local. Strangers cannot create canonical history, progress, career state, or public claims.

## 6. Activity Veil

Daily coding activity remains part of V4, but never as a contribution-grid clone or productivity scoreboard.

The world renders a transparent spatial layer called the **Activity Veil**:

- real public coding days create temporal density;
- factual commit counts may affect local atmosphere with a capped, non-linear mapping;
- active repositories create distinct strands without exposing private repository identity;
- pull requests, tags, releases, and curated milestones can become landmarks;
- routine commits aggregate into weather, rhythm, and residue rather than pretending to be achievements;
- code activity never implies learning progress, mastery, quality, hours worked, or career advancement.

The source and its coverage must always be explicit in the data contract. Incomplete public-provider data must never be presented as a complete record of Adham's work.

## 7. Truth and privacy laws

1. Every claim must be true.
2. Rendering may represent truth; rendering may never create truth.
3. Objective integrations may contribute typed facts. Human meaning remains curated.
4. Foundry's private canonical record projects only approved public events.
5. Private repository names, commits, curriculum detail, and drafts remain private unless explicitly published.
6. Absence of provider data means unknown or quiet—not failure and not zero effort.
7. Commits are code activity, not achievements.
8. Learning units are verified curriculum progress, not calendar days.
9. Meaningful events append to history; redesigns never erase them.
10. Visitor behavior is not identity state.

## 8. Cinematic journey

The default journey remains legible:

> **ADHAM → NOW → WORK → GROWTH / TIME → HISTORY → PRESENT → CONTACT**

It must feel like one movie rather than repeated editorial posters. Each chapter changes the world's material and meaning:

- **ADHAM / ORIGIN** — recognition; the monument is first encountered.
- **NOW** — the human and present direction resolve from the environment.
- **WORK** — projects become places with laws derived from real software.
- **GROWTH / TIME** — future structure is visible; evidence alters it only when it exists.
- **HISTORY** — previous work fossilizes, withdraws, and remains traversable.
- **PRESENT** — the system converges into current equilibrium, not conclusion.
- **CONTACT** — a practical surface earned after context.

The Index is a revelation and a tool: the world reorganizes into navigation architecture while semantic HTML remains direct, keyboard-operable, and obvious.

## 9. Motion, sound, and time

Motion communicates force, material, orientation, state, or emotion. Stillness is part of motion. Native input remains immediate; the world may carry inertia.

Sound is silent by default and opt-in. Every sound must materially improve an event. If it cannot pass that test, it is removed.

Time is not decorative chronology. Recent activity may energize and fade. Maturity may stabilize. Important changes become permanent landmarks. Old identities do not disappear; they acquire age.

## 10. Accessibility and performance

Hardware changes manifestation, never meaning.

- **FULL** receives the richest world simulation.
- **REDUCED** keeps causality with simpler matter.
- **STATIC-LOW** keeps the composition and state legible without continuous simulation.
- **REDUCED MOTION** keeps transformation understandable without forced movement.

Mobile is specifically directed, not a compressed desktop. Keyboard, focus, contrast, semantics, touch targets, and screen-reader structure are part of the flagship experience.

## 11. Definition of done

V4 is not done because it builds, deploys, or contains many effects. It is done only when:

- the first ten seconds are unmistakable and immediately belong to Adham;
- the world feels larger than the viewport and continues beyond the current frame;
- the identity system passes the delete test;
- the visitor's force feels local, physical, and consequential;
- every chapter has a unique material and emotional state while remaining one world;
- the Index reveals that the world is also the map;
- Work is encountered rather than inventoried;
- Growth makes truthful zero feel like unresolved potential;
- the Activity Veil makes real time felt without worshipping commit volume;
- History makes age physical;
- Present feels like convergence and unresolved future;
- ZeroUpload proves its boundary through a real browser-local operation;
- weak hardware, reduced motion, mobile, keyboard, and failure states retain the concept;
- no feature needs Adham standing beside the visitor to explain or apologize for it.

The final standard is not “excellent portfolio.”

> **A timeless, cinematic identity world that can still be true after Adham becomes someone materially different.**
